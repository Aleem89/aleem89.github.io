Franchise analytics warehouse

03

Scorecard app: 7.3 s → 0.2 s

A Streamlit scorecard for franchise owners was slow enough that people stopped opening it.

  • Precomputed revenue and GA4 attribution into materialized views and collapsed duplicate query passes: Overview 7.3 s → 0.2 s, master view 1.9 s → 35 ms.

Live territory metrics

  • Deleted 1,070 lines of ETL; counts are now current with under 10 ms of added query cost.
  • Reconciled the metric definitions with the client's finance team and documented them in an OpenMetadata glossary with lineage.
